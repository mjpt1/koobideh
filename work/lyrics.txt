[Intro – Spoken, Playful Male Voice]
Hey, everybody!
You want the secret to Persian delight?
Let’s make some Koobideh tonight!
Get your skewers ready… fire up the grill!
[Verse 1 – Ingredients]
Two pounds of lamb, or beef with some fat,
Three grated onions — now how about that?
Squeeze out the juice, don’t let it stay,
We want that meat to hold its shape!
Add salt and pepper, season it right,
A little turmeric if you like the bite.
Keep it simple, keep it real,
That’s how you start a Persian meal!
[Pre-Chorus – Build the Beat]
Mix it, mash it, work that meat,
Knead it till the texture’s smooth and neat!
Ten minutes strong, let the flavors unite,
Cover and chill it — let it rest overnight!
[Chorus – Catchy, Upbeat]
Koo-bi-deh! Koo-bi-deh!
Persian kebab is on the way!
On the skewer, on the flame,
Smoky, juicy, that’s the game!
Koo-bi-deh! Koo-bi-deh!
Turn it fast, don’t let it fall away!
Rice and butter, sumac too,
Persian barbecue is waiting for you!
[Verse 2 – Shaping the Skewers]
Wet your hands, now grab a ball,
Press it gently, spread it tall.
Place the meat on a flat metal skewer,
Shape it evenly, make it smoother.
Pinch the ridges with your thumb,
Make those classic grooves become!
Seal the edges, hold it tight,
Keep that meat upon the skewer right!
[Verse 3 – Fire and Grill]
Get your charcoal glowing red,
No big flames beneath the bread!
Place the skewers, side by side,
Turn them quickly, don’t let them slide.
Flip them often at the start,
That’s the secret, that’s the art!
Cook till golden, juicy inside,
No raw meat left when it’s time to dine!
[Bridge – Persian Feast]
Grilled tomatoes, charred and sweet,
Fresh herbs make the perfect treat.
Saffron rice in golden light,
Butter melting — what a sight!
Sprinkle sumac, squeeze some lime,
Gather ‘round, it’s dinner time!
From Tehran streets to lands afar,
Persian Koobideh’s the barbecue star!
[Final Chorus – Big Celebration]
Koo-bi-deh! Koo-bi-deh!
Made with love the Persian way!
Onions, meat, and charcoal heat,
Nothing beats this smoky treat!
Koo-bi-deh! Koo-bi-deh!
Bring the family, shout hooray!
Saffron rice and kebabs divine,
Persian flavor, every time!
[Outro – Spoken]
Remember the rule, now here’s the key:
Squeeze those onions, knead carefully!
Keep turning till the meat is done…
Persian Koobideh — now that’s fun!
